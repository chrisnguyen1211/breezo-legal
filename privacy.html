Breezo – Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 October 2026 · App: Breezo: AI Cleaner & Storage (com.breezo.cleaner)

Breezo helps you free up storage on your Android phone. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what leaves your device.

1. Data processed on your device only

  • Photos and videos (with your permission): read to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, and to compress files you choose. Analysis runs entirely on your phone.
  • Files in shared storage: sizes and names of large media files, so you can review and delete them.
  • Contacts (only if you open the Contacts cleaner and grant access): read to find duplicate or incomplete contacts, and changed only when you tap Merge, Save or Delete.
  • Secret Space: photos you hide are copied into the app’s private storage and protected with a PIN, which is stored as a salted hash.

None of this content — photos, videos, file names, contacts or your PIN — is uploaded, sold or shared. Deleting the app removes its private storage, including Secret Space; unhide items first if you want to keep them.

2. Data collected automatically

If crash reporting is enabled in a release, Breezo uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to receive crash reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version and an installation identifier. These reports contain no photos, contacts or file contents and are used only to fix bugs. Google Play may also provide aggregated install and stability statistics.

3. Permissions

Photos & videos — to scan and clean your gallery. Contacts — for the Contacts cleaner. Notifications — for optional junk reminders, which you can turn off in Settings. You can revoke any permission in Android Settings at any time.

4. Deletion

Items you clean are moved to the system “Recently deleted” trash on Android 11 and later, where they stay for 30 days before Android removes them. Contacts you delete are removed immediately.

5. Children

Breezo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
